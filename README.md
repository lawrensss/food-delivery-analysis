# 🍔 Food Delivery Analysis - New Delhi

## Overview
This project analyzes food delivery operations in New Delhi using a dataset of 10,000+ orders. The goal is to uncover business insights that can improve delivery efficiency, customer satisfaction, and revenue.

---

## Dataset
- **Source:** Food delivery orders from New Delhi restaurants
- **Rows:** 10,000+ orders
- **Features:** Order details, restaurant info, delivery times, payment methods, customer ratings

---

## Tools & Libraries
- **Python:** pandas, numpy, matplotlib, seaborn
- **Environment:** Google Colab
- **Visualization:** Custom charts and graphs for business insights

---

## Key Insights

### 📈 Order Patterns
- **Peak hours:** 12-2 PM (lunch) and 7-9 PM (dinner)
- **Busiest days:** Weekends account for 40% of weekly orders
- **Slowest days:** Monday and Tuesday have 30% fewer orders

### ⏱️ Delivery Performance
- **Average delivery time:** 35 minutes
- **20% of orders** take longer than 45 minutes
- **Fastest cuisine:** Pizza (28 min average)
- **Slowest cuisine:** Biryani (42 min average)

### 💳 Payment Analysis
- **Digital payments:** 65% of orders
- **Cash on delivery:** 35% of orders
- **Customer ratings:** Digital payments average 4.2/5 vs Cash at 3.8/5

### ⭐ Customer Satisfaction
- **Average rating:** 4.1/5
- **Top-rated cuisines:** South Indian (4.4), Chinese (4.3)
- **Lowest-rated:** Fast food (3.7)

### 💰 Revenue Insights
- **Average order value:** ₹450
- **Highest value days:** Saturday and Sunday
- **Peak revenue month:** December (holiday season)

## Business Recommendations

### 🚀 For Operations
1. **Staffing:** Increase delivery partners during peak hours (12-2pm, 7-9pm)
2. **Restaurant onboarding:** Prioritize North Indian, Chinese, and South Indian restaurants
3. **Delivery zones:** Investigate areas with consistently slow delivery times

### 📢 For Marketing
4. **Weekend promotions:** Target Fridays-Sundays with special offers
5. **Digital payments:** Offer small discounts for online payments to improve ratings
6. **Loyalty program:** Reward frequent customers to increase order frequency

### 🍽️ For Menu/Restaurants
7. **Cuisine spotlight:** Feature top-performing cuisines prominently
8. **Restaurant performance:** Share delivery time data with restaurants to improve
